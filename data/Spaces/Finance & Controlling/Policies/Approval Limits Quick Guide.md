# Approval limits – quick guide

Valid from 1 April 2026 (see *Delegation of Authority Matrix*, Executive Board space).

| What | You can approve | Above that |
|---|---|---|
| Purchase order, budgeted | Head of department ≤ EUR 100k | Executive Board member ≤ EUR 1 m |
| Purchase order, not budgeted | Head of department ≤ EUR 25k | Executive Board member ≤ EUR 250k |
| Expense claims | Line manager | Head of department for claims > EUR 2,500 |
| Supplier invoice without PO | not allowed | – ask Procurement for a retrospective PO |
| Credit notes to customers | Financial Controller ≤ EUR 50k | CFO |

Two-person rule for every payment. Bank detail changes: call-back to a known contact *before* the change (management letter 2025, point 3).
