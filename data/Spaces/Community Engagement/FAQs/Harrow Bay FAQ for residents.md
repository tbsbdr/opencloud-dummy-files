# Harrow Bay Offshore Wind – FAQ for residents

## What is being built?
40 Halden HTS-12 turbines (480 MW) 38 km off the coast, with two export cables landing at Harrow Bay East.

## Will I see the turbines?
On a clear day they are visible as small shapes on the horizon. Construction vessels are visible from the promenade.

## Why is the cable work taking longer?
One export cable failed a factory test and a 9 km section is being re-made (risk HB-RISK-017). The second cable will be laid later than planned; the first power date of September 2027 is not affected.

## Is piling noise harmful to porpoises?
Piling uses bubble curtains and soft-start, and is paused in sensitive seasons. Marlow Ecology monitors harbour porpoise and seabirds.

## Who do I contact?
community@weyland-energy.example or the fisheries liaison officer for sea users.
