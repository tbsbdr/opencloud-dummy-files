# Corporate PPA Market Note – after the Arcturus deal


## Context

On 12 May 2026 we signed WE-PPA-2026-004 with Arcturus Steel AG: 15 years, 150 MW pay-as-produced from Harrow Bay (~590 GWh/a), starting at Harrow Bay COD (planned 30 June 2028).


## What we learned

- Industrial buyers value **additionality** and long tenor more than the lowest price.
- Pay-as-produced was acceptable because Arcturus will firm volumes with its own flexibility and later with hydrogen.
- Credit support (parent guarantee) took longer to negotiate than the price.


## Pipeline implications

- Northfield: plan for up to 40 % corporate PPAs.
- Solar projects: hybrid PPAs with storage shaping (Millbrook model).
- Green Hydrogen Pilot strengthens the Arcturus relationship – possible second PPA.
