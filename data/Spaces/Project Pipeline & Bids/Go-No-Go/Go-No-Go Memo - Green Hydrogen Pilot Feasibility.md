# Go/No-Go Memo – Green Hydrogen Pilot (Feasibility)

*Decision: GO to feasibility – approved by Dr. Amara Okafor, 20 January 2026*


## Proposal

Run a feasibility study for a 5 MW PEM electrolyser at Port of Eastholm, with Arcturus Steel AG as hydrogen offtaker. Budget EUR 650,000, duration 6 months, lead Isabel Ferreira (Hydrogen Project Engineer).


## Why now

- Arcturus is already negotiating a 15-year PPA with us (later signed as WE-PPA-2026-004).
- EU Innovation Fund and national schemes fund first-of-a-kind pilots.
- Port of Eastholm is our Harrow Bay O&M base – land and grid available.


## Go/no-go criteria for the next gate

- LCOH with grant support below EUR 8/kg
- Signed MoU with Arcturus for offtake
- No safety showstopper (Seveso, setbacks) at the port site
