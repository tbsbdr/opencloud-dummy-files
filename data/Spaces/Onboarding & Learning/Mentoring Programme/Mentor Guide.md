# Mentor guide – 5 tips

1. **Let the mentee lead.** Ask what they want from each session; keep a short shared note.
2. **Ask more than you tell.** "What options do you see?" beats "I would do…".
3. **Share real stories** – the T-07 gearbox exchange, the first monopile, the Millbrook consultation evenings. Including what went wrong.
4. **Open doors.** Introduce your mentee to two people they would not meet otherwise.
5. **Stay confidential.** Nothing goes to the line manager without the mentee's agreement.
