# Lunch & Learn notes – How a PPA works (4 June 2026)

Speakers: Priya Nair (Legal & Contracts), Marcus Bell (Finance & Controlling) · 81 attendees (52 in the room, 29 on the stream)

## Key takeaways
- A PPA turns a volatile market price into a predictable revenue stream – which is what our lenders need.
- WE-PPA-2026-004 with Arcturus Steel: 150 MW of Harrow Bay, 15 years, **pay-as-produced** – Arcturus takes 31.25 % of whatever we produce.
- Baseload PPAs sound simpler for the buyer, but the seller carries the shaping risk – expensive for a wind farm.
- Guarantees of origin travel with the power; that is what allows Arcturus to sell "green steel".

## Questions from the audience
- *What happens if Harrow Bay is late?* – There is a long-stop date (end of 2029) and capped delay damages. The cable delay (HB-RISK-017) is within the buffer.
- *Can we sell the other 330 MW the same way?* – Partly CfD, partly merchant today; more corporate PPAs are possible.
- *Do we sign PPAs for Millbrook?* – Indicative offers are being evaluated, decision after planning consent.
