# We're hiring: SCADA Engineer

*Copenhagen*

**Weyland Energy** develops, builds and operates wind, solar and storage assets across Northern Europe – 1.1 GW in operation, 4.2 GW in the pipeline and 450 people in Hamburg, Edinburgh and Copenhagen. We are looking for a **SCADA Engineer** in Copenhagen.


## Your mission

- Own the SCADA and data pipeline for Kestrel Ridge and prepare the integration of Harrow Bay.
- Maintain OPC UA interfaces, alarms and historian; work with IT & Security on OT network segmentation.
- Build availability and performance dashboards with the performance team.


## You bring

- Degree in automation, computer science or electrical engineering.
- Experience with wind or solar SCADA systems, OPC UA, SQL and Python.
- Interest in IEC 62443 and OT security.


## Why Weyland

- Safety first – our HSE Golden Rules apply to everyone, every day.
- Flexible working, learning budget, pension and a team that helps each other.
