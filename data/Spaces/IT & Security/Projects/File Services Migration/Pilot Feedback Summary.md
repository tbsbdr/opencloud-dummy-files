# Pilot feedback – file services on OpenCloud

Pilot: 7 Sep – 18 Sep 2026 · 38 users (IT & Security, HR & People, Company Handbook editors) · 31 survey answers

**Overall: 4.4 / 5** – 27 of 31 want to keep working on the new platform after the pilot.

## What people liked
- "Spaces finally match how we work – I see HR, the Handbook and my personal files, nothing else."
- Search finds text *inside* documents (e.g. a salary band in a spreadsheet, a clause in a works council agreement).
- Version history: two users restored an overwritten spreadsheet themselves without a ticket.
- Share links with expiry and password for externals; very easy to see who has access.
- ODF documents (.odt/.ods) open and preview without conversion.
