# Strategy day with the Supervisory Board – 20 January 2026

Hotel Hafen Hamburg, 09:00–17:00. Moderation: Amara. Notes: Ruth Adebayo.

## Morning – where do we stand?
- Karin: "We are no longer a developer, we are an operator that develops." Liked the framing.
- Gareth pushed hard on offshore follow-on sites – wants proof that we can deliver Harrow Bay first. Agreed: no second offshore FID before Harrow Bay first power.
- Ulrike: funding is the bottleneck, not the pipeline. 4.2 GW is fine as a pipeline number, but the plan must show what we can finance.

## Afternoon – choices
1. **2.3 or 2.5 GW by 2030?** Majority for 2.5 GW if capital recycling is part of the plan. Jonas to model 49 % stake sales of German onshore portfolio.
2. **Hydrogen** – pilot yes, scale-up only with Arcturus as anchor customer and grant funding (EU Innovation Fund).
3. **Merchant exposure** – max. 30 % of group revenue unhedged from 2028. Long-term PPAs (Arcturus model) are the preferred route.
4. **Tagline** – everyone liked "Clean power, built to last." Noah to check with brand.
