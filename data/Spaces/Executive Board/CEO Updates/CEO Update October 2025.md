# CEO Update – October 2025

*My first month as CEO*

Dear members of the Supervisory Board,

one month into the role, I want to share what I have seen and heard. I visited all three offices, Kestrel Ridge and the Port of Eastholm, and held 41 one-to-one conversations.


## Business performance

October revenue was EUR 29.9 m, bringing the year to date to EUR 247.7 m. The full-year forecast of EUR 311 m revenue and EUR 147 m EBITDA is confirmed.


## What I heard

- Pride in Kestrel Ridge and in Harrow Bay – people want to be part of it.
- Too many approval steps; a project manager needs four signatures for a EUR 50k order.
- Edinburgh feels far from Hamburg – decisions take too long.
- Safety culture is strong on site, weaker in contractor management.


## First actions

- New delegation of authority matrix by April 2026
- Monthly leadership call with all heads of department
- Strategy 2030 process with the board (workshop on 20 January 2026)

Kind regards,  
Amara
