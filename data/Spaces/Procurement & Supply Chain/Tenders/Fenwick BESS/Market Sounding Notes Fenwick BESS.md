*Mateo Rossi / Laura Kessler · last update 17 Sep 2026*

Project: Fenwick Battery Storage, 100 MW / 200 MWh, next to the 132 kV Fenwick substation.
Grid offer expected Q2 2027 – no tender before that, this is only market sounding.

## Open questions
- Cell origin and traceability (Modern Slavery Act / LkSG) – need audit rights down to cell level
- Fire safety: UL 9540A test reports, spacing requirements from the fire service
- Grid-forming capability – NordGrid Transmission may require it from 2028
