How to set up your e-mail signature
===================================

Outlook (Windows/Mac): open Email Signature.html in a browser, select all, copy, and paste into Settings > Signatures. Then replace the fields in brackets.

Thunderbird: Account Settings > 'Use HTML' > paste the file content into the signature box.
