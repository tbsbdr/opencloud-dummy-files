# Legal & Contracts space

**Owner:** Priya Nair (Head of Legal & Contracts) · Lukas Meyer (Legal Counsel) · Emma Wilson (Paralegal)

- `Contract Register/` – the single list of all contracts. Every signed contract gets a WE-CON number from Emma.
- `PPAs/` – power purchase agreements, one folder per PPA (e.g. WE-PPA-2026-004 Arcturus Steel).
- `Project Contracts/` – summaries, notices and correspondence for key project contracts.
- `NDAs/`, `Templates/` – please always start from the templates; the NDA template is shared with all staff.
- `Corporate/` – commercial register, shareholder resolutions, powers of attorney.
- `Compliance/`, `Data Protection/`, `Litigation/` – restricted.

Signed originals are kept in the Hamburg document safe. Scans go into the matching `Scans` folder.
