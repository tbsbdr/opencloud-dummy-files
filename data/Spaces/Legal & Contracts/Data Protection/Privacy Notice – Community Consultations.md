# Privacy notice – community consultations

**Controller:** Weyland Energy GmbH, Am Sandtorkai 41, 20457 Hamburg, Germany (UK representative: Weyland Energy UK Ltd, Edinburgh).
**Data Protection Officer:** dpo@weyland-energy.example

## What we collect
When you attend a consultation event (for example for Millbrook Solar & Storage), fill in a feedback form or sign up for project updates, we collect your name, postcode, email address (optional) and your comments.

## Why
- To consider your feedback and report it to the planning authority in our Statement of Community Involvement (legal basis: public interest / legitimate interest).
- To send you project updates if you asked us to (consent – you can withdraw at any time).

## How long
Feedback is kept until two years after the planning decision. Newsletter data is deleted when you unsubscribe.

## Your rights
You can ask for access, correction, erasure, restriction, or object to processing. You may also complain to the ICO or to the Hamburg data protection authority.
