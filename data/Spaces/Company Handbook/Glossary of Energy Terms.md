# Glossary of energy terms

Our industry loves abbreviations. This list explains the ones you will hear in your first weeks – with examples from our own projects.


## A

**AEP** – Annual energy production – expected yearly output of a wind or solar farm, usually stated as P50 (median) and P90.  
**Array cable** – Medium-voltage cable (at Harrow Bay 66 kV) connecting turbines to the offshore substation.  
**Availability** – Share of time an asset is able to produce. Kestrel Ridge target 97.0 %, achieved 97.4 % in 2025.  

## B

**BESS** – Battery energy storage system, e.g. 20 MW / 40 MWh at Millbrook or 100 MW / 200 MWh at Fenwick. MW is power, MWh is energy.  
**BNG** – Biodiversity net gain – measurable improvement of habitats compared to before the project (Millbrook: 20 %).  
**Bubble curtain** – Curtain of air bubbles around a monopile during piling that reduces underwater noise for harbour porpoises.  

## C

**Capacity factor** – Actual generation divided by the maximum possible generation at full capacity. Kestrel Ridge 2025: 31.2 %.  
**CfD** – Contract for Difference – government-backed price support: the generator receives the difference between a strike price and the market price.  
**COD** – Commercial operation date. Harrow Bay is planned for 30 June 2028.  
**Curtailment** – Reduction of output ordered by the grid operator or due to negative prices.  

## E

**EPC / EPCI** – Engineering, procurement and construction (and installation) – a contract where one contractor delivers a complete scope.  
**Export cable** – High-voltage cable bringing power to shore. Harrow Bay: 2 × 220 kV, 62 km each.  

## F

**FID** – Final investment decision – the point when the project is fully financed and construction contracts are released (Harrow Bay: 12 Feb 2026).  
**Floating wind** – Turbines on floating foundations moored to the seabed, for water depths beyond ~ 60 m.  

## G

**GoO** – Guarantee of origin – certificate proving that 1 MWh was produced from renewable sources.  
**GWO** – Global Wind Organisation – sets basic safety training standards for the wind industry.  

## H

**Hub height** – Height of the rotor centre above sea level or ground. HTS-12: 140 m.  

## J

**Jack-up vessel** – Installation vessel that lowers legs to the seabed and lifts itself out of the water, e.g. MV Sea Atlas.  

## L

**LCOE** – Levelised cost of energy – lifetime cost per MWh produced.  

## M

**Monopile** – Single large steel tube driven into the seabed as a turbine foundation (Harrow Bay: 9.5 m diameter).  
**MW / MWh / MWp** – Megawatt (power), megawatt hour (energy), megawatt peak (solar module rating under standard conditions).  

## O

**O&M** – Operations and maintenance.  
**OSS** – Offshore substation – collects array cables and steps up voltage for export (HB-OSS, topside installed August 2026).  

## P

**P50 / P90** – Production levels exceeded with 50 % / 90 % probability. Lenders size debt on P90.  
**PPA** – Power purchase agreement – long-term contract to sell electricity, e.g. WE-PPA-2026-004 with Arcturus Steel (150 MW, 15 years).  
**Pay-as-produced** – PPA volume structure where the buyer takes a fixed share of whatever the asset actually produces.  
**PEM electrolyser** – Proton exchange membrane electrolyser that splits water into hydrogen and oxygen using electricity (Green Hydrogen Pilot: 5 MW).  

## R

**Rotor diameter** – Diameter of the circle swept by the blades. HTS-12: 236 m.  

## S

**SCADA** – Supervisory control and data acquisition – the system that monitors and controls turbines and substations.  
**Single-axis tracker** – Solar mounting system that turns modules east to west during the day.  

## T

**TP** – Transition piece – connects the monopile to the turbine tower and carries the boat landing.  

## W

**Wake loss** – Energy lost because turbines downstream sit in the slower, turbulent air behind other turbines.
