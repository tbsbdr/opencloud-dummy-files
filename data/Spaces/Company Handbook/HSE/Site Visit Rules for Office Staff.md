# Site visit rules for office staff (WE-HSE-PRC-021)

Visiting Kestrel Ridge, Port of Eastholm, the Harrow Bay onshore substation or – rarely – an offshore asset? Great. Please plan ahead:

1. **Register 48 hours in advance** with the site manager (Kestrel Ridge: Ben Okoye) or the marine coordination (Harrow Bay: Ingrid Solberg).
2. **Induction:** watch the 20-minute site induction video and pass the short quiz before you travel.
3. **PPE:** safety boots (S3), helmet with chin strap, high-vis jacket, safety glasses, gloves. Loan PPE is available at Kestrel Ridge and Eastholm – tell us your sizes.
4. **Escort:** visitors without GWO training stay with their escort at all times and do not climb turbines.
5. **Offshore:** only with BOSIET (or GWO Sea Survival), a valid offshore medical and a project director approval. No exceptions.
6. **Weather:** Scottish upland weather changes fast. Bring warm, waterproof clothes – even in July.
7. **Photos:** fine for internal use, but no photos of people without asking, and no posting on social media before Marketing has checked.

Golden Rule #1 applies to you as well: if something feels unsafe, stop and ask.
