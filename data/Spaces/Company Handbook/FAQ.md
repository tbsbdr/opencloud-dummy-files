# Frequently asked questions

**Where do I find my payslip?**
In the HR self-service portal under *Documents*. UK colleagues: from October 2026 in the Thistle Payroll portal.

**How do I book holiday?**
In the HR system. Your manager approves; the team calendar updates automatically. Holiday calendar 2027 is in the HR space and the Handbook.

**Can I work from abroad?**
Up to 20 working days per year in the EU/UK after HR approval – see the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy.

**I want to visit Kestrel Ridge or Harrow Bay. What do I need?**
Read *HSE/Site Visit Rules for Office Staff* and register 48 h in advance. Offshore visits need BOSIET and an offshore medical.

**How do I share a file with a partner (e.g. Halden Turbine Systems)?**
Use a share link from the project space with an expiry date. For confidential files add a password. Never email large attachments or use private cloud accounts.

**Who do I call when my laptop breaks?**
IT Service Desk: ext. 444, servicedesk@weyland-energy.example, or walk in on the 3rd floor in Hamburg (Tue–Thu mornings).

**What is the Lunch & Learn?**
A 45-minute session every second Thursday. The library (e.g. *Floating Offshore Wind 101*, *How a PPA works*) is in the Onboarding & Learning space.

**Where are the company templates and logos?**
In the Brand Assets space.

**Is there a company bike scheme?**
Yes – bike leasing in Germany, cycle to work in the UK, bike allowance in Denmark. See the Benefits Overview.
