# Lessons learned – Annual Report 2025

Retro on 14 April 2026 with Jonas, Marcus, Ravi, Leah, Fiona.

## What went well
- In-house layout worked – saved ~EUR 38k, and Ravi knew the content.
- One shared space for drafts, proofs and comments instead of e-mail attachments – nobody worked on an old version.
- Versions of the CEO letter made it easy to show Amara what changed between drafts.
- Published on time (26 March), 96 pages, accessible PDF.

## What didn't
- Operations chapter late because of the T-07 incident – we should have a backup author.
- 62 review comments in round 1 – too many reviewers on the same proof. Next year: one consolidated review per chapter.
- Photo releases collected too late (two technicians had left the site).

## For the Annual Report 2026
- Kick-off in October, not November.
- Harrow Bay construction photos from the summer campaign (monopiles, topside) – ask Elena for drone footage.
- Start the sustainability chapter together with the Sustainability Report 2026 team (Hannah).
