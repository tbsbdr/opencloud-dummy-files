Boilerplate and key messages
============================

BOILERPLATE (long)

About Weyland Energy. Weyland Energy GmbH develops, builds and operates wind, solar and storage assets across Northern Europe. Founded in 2011 and headquartered in Hamburg with offices in Edinburgh and Copenhagen, the company employs around 450 people, operates 1,100 MW of renewable capacity and has a development pipeline of 4.2 GW. In 2025 its assets avoided 1.2 million tonnes of CO₂. Clean power, built to last.

BOILERPLATE (short, 280 characters)

Weyland Energy develops, builds and operates wind, solar and storage assets in Northern Europe. Hamburg-based, ~450 people, 1,100 MW in operation, 4.2 GW pipeline.
