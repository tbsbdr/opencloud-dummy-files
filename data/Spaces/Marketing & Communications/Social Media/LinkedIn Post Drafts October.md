## Mon 5 Oct – Harrow Bay progress

14 down, 26 to go. Since 7 April the team on MV Sea Atlas has installed 14 of the 40 monopile foundations for Harrow Bay Offshore Wind – each 9.5 m wide and driven into the seabed behind a double bubble curtain to protect harbour porpoises. Thank you to everyone at the Port of Eastholm. #PoweringTheNorth #OffshoreWind
