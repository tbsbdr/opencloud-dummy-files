# Fenwick – Route-to-Market Options

*Notes by Kwame Mensah, shared by Liam McAllister – for discussion*


## Revenue streams in scope

- **Frequency response** – Dynamic Containment, Dynamic Moderation, Dynamic Regulation (day-ahead auctions, EFA blocks).
- **Wholesale arbitrage** – day-ahead and intraday trading.
- **Balancing Mechanism** – bids/offers to the system operator.
- **Capacity market** – T-4 auction, 2-hour de-rating factor ~21 %, up to 15-year agreements for new build.
- **Stability services** – upside only, requires grid-forming PCS.


## Contract structures

| Structure | Typical tenor | Lender view |
|---|---|---|
| Merchant + optimiser | 2–3 years, rolling | Low gearing (≈ 40 %) |
| Revenue floor | 5–10 years | Gearing on floor (≈ 55–60 %) |
| Tolling | 7–10 years | Highest gearing (≈ 70 %) |


## Preference

Revenue floor with upside share, combined with a 15-year capacity market agreement. Final decision at FID (2028).
