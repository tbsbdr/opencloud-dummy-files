# Fenwick – Load Flow Pre-Study Notes

*Ahmed Rahimi / Mei Tanaka, reviewed by Sofie Lindqvist*


## Model

Public network model (2025 winter peak and summer minimum), Fenwick 132 kV with 2 × 132 kV circuits to the north.


## Results

- Export 100 MW at summer minimum: circuit loading 87 % (N-0), 118 % (N-1) → constraint under outage conditions.
- Import 100 MW at winter peak: 74 % (N-0), 101 % (N-1).
- Voltage step change on full trip < 3 % – acceptable.


## Implication

Expect either a reinforcement (circuit re-conductoring) or a non-firm connection with an intertrip scheme. Non-firm operation would reduce wholesale arbitrage revenue by an estimated 3–5 %; frequency response is largely unaffected. Modelled as a downside sensitivity in the revenue stack.
