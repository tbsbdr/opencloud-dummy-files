# Harrow Bay Offshore Wind

480 MW offshore wind farm, 38 km off the coast of Harrow Bay in the UK North Sea.
40 × Halden HTS-12 turbines on monopiles · offshore substation HB-OSS · 2 × 220 kV export cables.

**Status (2 October 2026):** in construction – 14 of 40 monopiles installed, offshore substation
topside installed on 19 August 2026. First power planned for 30 September 2027, COD 30 June 2028.
