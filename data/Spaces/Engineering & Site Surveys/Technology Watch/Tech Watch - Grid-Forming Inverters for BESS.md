# Tech Watch: Grid-Forming Inverters for BESS


## Summary

Grid-forming (GFM) inverters behave like a voltage source and can provide synthetic inertia and system strength. GB system operator stability markets already pay for this. For Fenwick Battery Storage (100 MW / 200 MWh) a GFM-capable design could add a revenue stream on top of frequency response, arbitrage and capacity market.


## Points to watch

- Most tier-1 BESS integrators now offer GFM as an option; cost premium 3–6 % of PCS cost.
- Oversizing of the PCS (1.2–1.5 × short-term current) may be needed for fault current contribution.
- Compliance testing is new – grid operators' test procedures are still evolving.
- Interaction with nearby wind farms (e.g. control interactions) must be studied.


## Action

Make GFM capability a scored criterion in the Fenwick integrator RFP (see WE-ENG-GDL-007 Rev 1).
